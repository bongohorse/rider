# Build your own vehicle

## Idea

Players build their own vehicle from parts and drive it on the normal tracks. Inspiration: Bad Piggies, Main Assembly, Trailmakers, Hill Climb Racing's tuning screen — but simple enough for a phone.

## Why it is fun

- Every player solves a track differently ("I built a long low car for the gap").
- Fits the toy look (parts like plastic toy pieces or wooden blocks).
- Gives the physics engine a second purpose besides driving and makes sharing interesting (share a vehicle code, race its ghost).
- Strong pairing with the track editor: build a track and the vehicle that beats it.

## Smallest playable version

- A **grid workshop** (e.g. 8 × 5 cells) in the gym.
- 6 part types: frame block, wheel (2 sizes), motor, spring strut, seat with rider (required, carries the head sensor), ballast weight.
- Rules: exactly one seat, at least two wheels, all parts connected, limited part budget.
- "Drive" button spawns the vehicle at a gym station; "Edit" returns to the workshop.
- Save/load locally as JSON; share as a short code (same mechanism as track codes).

## Technical approach (plausible, not verified)

- **Data first:** a versioned vehicle file (parts, grid positions, connections), validated like presets and stations. The current buggy becomes the first built-in vehicle in this format — this alone is useful even without a player-facing builder.
- **Physics:** one dynamic body per rigid group of frame blocks (shapes added with `b2d.body.create_shape`), wheels as separate bodies on wheel joints (motor, spring), rigid connections via weld joints only where groups must stay separate. Fewer bodies = more stable and cheaper.
- **Driving:** the existing tick owner and feel layer drive all motor wheels; lean torque applies to the seat's body. Feel values become per-vehicle data.
- **Visuals:** parts are sprites generated by the existing art generator, so sizes always match collision shapes.
- **Reset/ownership:** spawn and despawn the whole vehicle through the same generation-checked reset contract as stations.

## Risks

- **Stability:** player-built vehicles can create joint chains that jitter or explode in Box2D. Mitigation: grid snapping, few joint types, merge rigid groups into one body, mass ratio limits between connected parts.
- **Fair time trials:** custom vehicles make medal times incomparable. Keep official medals on fixed vehicles; custom vehicles get their own leaderboard/category or a free-play mode.
- **Phone UI:** building with fingers is hard. Large cells, drag from a bottom tray, pinch zoom, undo.
- **Scope creep:** easy to grow into a second game. Ship the small version first and measure whether players use it.
- **Performance:** cap part count; measure physics step time with the largest allowed vehicle on the reference phone.

## Open questions

- Building as a core feature, or a bonus mode after the main game works?
- Free building, or only swapping parts on fixed frames (much simpler, still personal)?
- Does every track allow custom vehicles, or only special tracks?

## Prerequisites before it could be scheduled

- Phase B fun acceptance passed (driving itself is fun).
- Vehicle data format (can be done earlier, see above).
- Track/station format and sharing codes exist (Wave 2 in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md)).
- A second fixed vehicle (motorbike, Wave 4) has proven that vehicles are truly data-driven.
