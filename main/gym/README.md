## World and terrain

- `/main/gym/boot.collection` boots; its proxy owns the `gym` world, initialized only after `proxy_loaded` (compatible with A20 proxy time scaling). `controller.script` is the sole physics owner. G1 stays `/main/main.collection` (`build --fixture physics`).
- Lengths are engine units; `physics.scale = 0.01` (100 units = 1 m). The chain binding scales vertices and ghost vertices itself: do not scale twice.
- A collision resource must contain a shape. In the safe initial update the owner removes the seed shape (index 1), verifies the body, then creates the chain and asserts the new shape is a chain segment. Shape indices change on creation (pitfall 4).
- Terrain chains enable `enable_sensor_events`; Box2D excludes shapes without it from sensor overlaps.
- Camera: orthographic Auto Fit; render-dt exponential following, zoom 1 → 0.82 with speed; it never rotates with the chassis. After Super Mario World ([Keren, "Scroll Back", GDC 2015](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2015/presentations/Keren_Itay_ScrollBack.pdf), slides 24 and 42; `feel.camera`):
  - x: velocity look-ahead of twice the preset's look time and maximum (A: 0.44 s, max 260 u) on the smoothed speed, with the smoothing lag led, so at speed the view really is 2.6 m ahead (before Claudio's 2026-10-20 request the lag ate it up: about 0.2 m at 6.6 m/s). Dual forward focus: the side switches only after 0.6 m of travel against it and then eases over (3/s), so a short roll back keeps the view. Hard side edges keep the chassis centre 120 u inside the view.
  - y: signed vertical velocity is smoothed at 1.5/s; speeds below 40 u/s have no look-ahead. Above that deadband the look-ahead grows with speed using the same preset time/cap as x (A: 0.44 s, max 260 u). Between 40 and 300 u/s a smooth blend yields from the ground window to movement framing; at 300 u/s it follows the chassis plus vertical look-ahead and smoothing-lag compensation. Vertical pans use 60% of the horizontal response rate, with matching lag compensation, so short crests/landings ease in while sustained descents keep their look-ahead. The upward window never overrides descent focus. Rotation and wheel contact do not select the look direction.
  - At rest the ground window still suppresses ±40 u of suspension/waves. Once realignment starts, its ground reference also eases at 1.5/s rather than copying every chassis height change. Its height reserves this window inside the bottom safety margin, so edge clamping does not move the camera on every bump. Final-position limits keep the chassis centre 100 u inside the top/bottom and 120 u inside the sides; very small views proportionally reduce margins rather than disabling limits. No world-coordinate camera floor or terrain-bound clamp prevents descent focus.
  - A start (first, Retry, section) uses the same vertical framing and edge limits. `a6` checks the horizontal focus threshold/steady look-ahead/reversal, deep fall/landing, four diagonal and pure vertical directions on desktop, wide phone, portrait and small views, plus quiet suspension and reduced vertical pan speed/acceleration over repeated crests.
- Checkerboard art is procedural test art.

## Vehicle and controls

- Three Defold-owned bodies (40 kg chassis, two 4 kg wheels, radius 22 u) and two wheel joints in the gym world. `vehicle.lua` applies all writes via the sole owner; input only records held actions.
- Commands are normalized throttle/brake/lean plus `allow_reverse`. Brake wins over throttle. With throttle released, speed below 8 u/s for 0.25 s latches reverse (8 rad/s) until brake release; retry clears the latch.
- Motors: active throttle/brake call `wake_bodies` first (setters do not wake, pitfall 2). Torque caps are binding-input units (1800 drive / 3000 brake), scaled once by the binding.
- Lean: A/D apply raw chassis torque (body torque is not scaled, pitfall 3): air assistance 48/72/96 N m (presets A/B/C, soft rate threshold 8/10/12 rad/s) and separate ground pitch assistance 120/180/240 N m (2.5 rad/s); either wheel contact selects ground. Thresholds stop adding same-direction torque; counter-steering stays possible. Not a moving centre of mass.
- Air pitch damping (#211, optional preset key `air_pitch_damping`, N m s, 0–20, default 0 = off; only F sets it): in flight (no wheel and no chassis contact) with lean exactly 0, `vehicle.apply` adds raw chassis torque −k·ω (ω in rad/s, unscaled). It never acts on the reset tick, on a sleeping chassis or at |ω| ≤ 0.01 rad/s. With tilt it acts only inside the dead zone. It stops the neutral nose-dive after a lip (A19c) without touching full-lean rotation. `v.damped`/`v.damped_ticks` and the air report's per-jump `damped_ticks` are read-only diagnostics.
- Wheel targets A/B/C: 32/64/96 rad/s (7.04/14.08/21.12 m/s theoretical); slider range 8–140 rad/s. Actual top rolling speed is capped near 10 m/s by Box2D's rotation limit ([#114](https://github.com/bongohorse/rider/issues/114)).
- Coasting (A13a): neutral grounded wheels keep zero-speed motors with `coast_torque` (default 400) plus wheel-only `rolling_resistance` (0.15 N m s). Target: 5 m/s to below 0.1 m/s within 6–15 m; a brake tap settles within two seconds.
- HUD/report speeds are m/s and km/h; history keeps engine units.
- **Pause and slow motion (A20):** Space or the Pause button pauses and resumes; X or Speed cycles 1x → 0.5x → 0.25x (touch: toolbar Pause / Speed). The loader sets the world proxy's time step after the owner has cleared input; while paused nothing ticks, drive/Retry/section/preset input is dropped, the engine sound is silent and a held key needs a new press after Resume. Slow motion is a time scale with smaller physics steps (1/120, 1/240 s), not a replay; FINISH and attempt times are world time, FPS uses engine time. Station switches, Edit and Play start at 1.0; values captured at another factor are marked not comparable (telemetry `time_factor`/`time_scaled_ticks`, station report `comparable`, feedback copy). Rules: [physics contract](../../docs/contracts/physics.md#pause-slow-motion-and-live-tuning).

## Record and replay

A21 ([#36](https://github.com/bongohorse/rider/issues/36)): the same run with two presets visible side by side. Rules: [physics contract](../../docs/contracts/physics.md#command-playback-and-ghosts).

- **Use:** B (or **Record [B]**; touch **Record run**) retries the station and records from its start; B again, any reset (R, P, V, bounds, head) or the 60 s cap ends the recording, which is kept. Switch the preset with P or the preset chips (same world, the recording survives), then G (or **Replay [G]**; touch **Replay run**) retries and replays the recorded commands on the current preset while a translucent ghost shows the recorded run. G again or any reset stops the replay. The status line shows the progress, `dx` (live minus ghost chassis x) and at the end the maximum deviation and a verdict.
- **Owner:** `replay.lua`, state on the controller instance (`self.replay`, never posted; only a short status line goes to the HUD). The replay is one more command source in `fixed_update`, ahead of live input: exactly one recorded command per tick through `vehicle.apply`; live drive input is ignored and held keys are cleared at its start and end. No second loop, no manual stepping.
- **Recording format, version 1** (A22 builds on it): `version`, `station`, `section` (id or false), `start_speed` (m/s), `preset`, `modified`, `tuning` (the 22 required `feel.preset_keys` values, plus an optional key such as `air_pitch_damping` only when it is not at its default, so A recordings are unchanged) and its `identity` string, `source_sha`; per tick k (1-based; tick 1 is the fixed tick whose reset ran the station start) `throttle[k]` and `brake[k]` (0/1), `reverse[k]` (`allow_reverse` as 0/1), `lean[k]` (−1…1) and `pose[9(k−1)+1…9k]`: chassis x, y, angle, rear wheel x, y, angle, front wheel x, y, angle in metres and radians, read after the tick's reset/start and before its command is applied. `trailing` adds pose `ticks+1`, the post-step pose of the last tick, read at the next fixed tick (before its reset), so the final comparison and the ghost's last pose include the last command. `scaled_ticks` counts ticks not at 1.0, `ended` the reason, `error` a refused recording. Cap 3600 ticks (60 s).
- **Tick policy:** one entry per fixed tick by construction; a missing or repeated tick (checked against the fixed tick counter) invalidates the recording and replay is refused; the player stops on a gap. Out-of-order entries cannot be stored. A replay needs the same station, section and start speed; a station switch, Edit/Play or import reloads the world and discards the recording (station switch and Edit/Play tested; import uses the same reload, not tested separately).
- **Comparison and tolerances (declared before running):** each replayed tick compares the live chassis pose with the recorded pose of the same tick (angle difference wrapped to ±π). Same preset and tuning: MATCH if position ≤ 0.05 m and angle ≤ 0.05 rad on every tick, else DIVERGED; another preset: COMPARED (the deviation is the result). Same preset means the same preset values (22 required plus the optional ones, #211), camera keys included, so a camera-only slider change also gives COMPARED. A replay ended by a crash reset (bounds, head) keeps its result and shows the tick it reached; with the same preset a crash before the recording's last command is DIVERGED. Any later reset clears a finished replay and its ghost. Ticks at a slow-motion factor in the recording or the replay, or live tuning during either, make it NOT COMPARABLE.
- **Pause and slow motion:** no fixed ticks while paused, so recorder, player and ghost freeze; B/G presses are dropped like other drive keys. Slow motion is allowed and marked not comparable.
- **Regression traces (A22):** see [Regression traces (A22)](#regression-traces-a22).
- **Ghost:** sprite-only game objects (`ghost_chassis.go` with the chassis and rider sprites, two `ghost_wheel.go`), tint alpha 0.4, behind the live vehicle; 4 sprites while a replay exists, no collision object or body. It shows the recorded pose of the tick after the current one, i.e. the post-step pose the live vehicle shows.

## Regression traces (A22)

[#37](https://github.com/bongohorse/rider/issues/37): CI detects unintended changes of jump distance or top speed. Debug case `a22` (CI group `regress`); definitions, tolerances and the check: `tools/gym/regress_spec.cjs`; runner `tools/gym/regress.cjs`; Lua `regress.lua` (loaded for `a22` only).

- **Traces** (`tests/regress/traces/*.json`), all on preset A, unmodified (the measurement baseline), at 1.0 and 60 Hz from a station start: `gaps-3` (Gaps section 3 at 7 m/s, A19 full throttle, 180 ticks: jump distance), `straight-top` (Straight from rest, full throttle, 600 ticks: top speed) and `air-big-back` (Air `big` at 7 m/s, A19c protocol with lean +1 held in flight, 420 ticks: takeoff, airtime, rotation, landing). Format: the A21 recording format version 1 as exported by the a22 recorder, plus `format`, `name`, `derivation` and `recorded` fields; derivation: poses rounded to 1e-6 m and rad. `tests/regress/expected.json` holds each trace's SHA-256 and the outcomes measured while recording, with the recording build SHA and browser.
- **Replay:** the test page injects the trace before engine start; `replay.install` validates the whole trace (version 1; exactly one throttle/brake/reverse/lean entry per tick; one pose per tick plus the trailing pose; finite values in range; 0 scaled ticks; no error) and `regress.lua` refuses a trace that is not preset A unmodified. The A21 player then replays it: one recorded command per fixed tick through `vehicle.apply`, no second loop, no manual stepping. Record and check start at the same point: 30 fixed ticks after vehicle-ready with neutral commands, then the normal record/replay Retry.
- **Outcomes:** top speed = the highest chassis vx at the start of a run tick, checked on every trace (top-speed coverage comes from `straight-top`; on `gaps-3` it is the 7 m/s start speed and on `air-big-back` the fall speed); Gaps: the section's status, jump distance, takeoff speed and peak landing compression (A19 observer); Air: the lip jump's status, takeoff speed, airtime, rotation, landing angle and peak compression (A19c observer). All are read one tick after the last command and must be final (`done`/`complete`) by then.
- **Frozen tolerances** (declared before the first recording, pinned by `tests/ci/regress.test.cjs`): A21 pose check MATCH (chassis ≤ 0.05 m and ≤ 0.05 rad on every tick), all commands applied, 0 mismatches, 0 scaled ticks; top speed 0.02 m/s (the a19/a19b/a19c speed repeat tolerance); Gaps (a19): jump 0.05 m, takeoff 0.02 m/s, compression 5 mm, same status; Air (a19c): takeoff 0.02 m/s, airtime 0.034 s, rotation 5°, landing angle 5°, compression 5 mm, same status.
- **Must fail:** the `gaps-3` trace with throttle released on ticks 1–45 (poses and expectations unchanged) must fail `verdict` and `takeoff_speed_mps`; `straight-top` replayed after a debug-only change of preset A's wheel target from 32 to 31 rad/s (live-tuning path, before the replay) must fail `top_speed_mps`.
- **Limits:** HTML5 debug build in headless Playwright Chromium (the CI browser) only; 60 Hz physics at time factor 1.0; render checked at 60 FPS and, for `gaps-3`, 30 FPS (two ticks per frame). Same build and browser replay bit-identically in practice (deviation at the 1e-6 pose rounding), but no cross-platform, native, Android or device determinism is claimed.
- **Deliberate regeneration only:** when a tuning or physics change is intended, run `python tools/gym/smoke.py --bundle <debug-bundle> --output <dir> --case a22 --record`. It records every trace with its protocol driver, rewrites `tests/regress/`, prints an old → new outcome table and then runs the full check on the new files, which must PASS. CI never regenerates (`regress.cjs` refuses `--record` when `CI` is set; `tests/ci/regress.test.cjs` rejects `--record` in any workflow). Review rule: a PR that changes `tests/regress/` names the intended change, pastes that table, and the reviewer confirms every changed outcome is intended; tolerances (`regress_spec.cjs`) never change in the same PR.

## Retry and recovery

- R queues one generation-tagged request; duplicates coalesce. The owner consumes it outside contacts: clears held commands, keeps the three bodies, destroys both joints (old handles invalid), disables/re-enables the bodies to clear old contacts, restores canonical pose and zero velocities on **both** GO and body (dynamic GO transforms overwrite body-only resets, pitfall 1), wakes bodies and recreates the joints. Terrain is unchanged; no collection reload.
- Still-held keys do not resume driving; a fresh press does. Camera, history, metrics and UI reason reset.
- Safety bounds come from each station file. Leaving them uses the same reset path with a reason label. No camera-exit, inactivity, stuck or timer reset.
- The first station start after attach calls the same `vehicle.reset` before applying the authored speed.

## Head sensor

A placeholder rider (`tools/art/rider.py`) has a script-created zero-density sensor circle at chassis-local (0, 56), radius 12 u; no ragdoll or solid head. Own bodies are excluded from overlaps. C or the overlay toggles the crash rule (default off: display only, red rider; on: reason-labelled retry). Overlaps are read in `fixed_update`; after a retry two completed-step reads are skipped. The filter table needs all three fields including `group_index = 0`.

## Rider visual reaction

The rider sprite is a child GO of the chassis pivoting at the hip (A15a). The owner only reads chassis velocity/spin changes in chassis space; each frame applies them as kicks to a damped spring per axis (`feel.rider`: 3 Hz, damping 0.55): acceleration leans back, braking leans forward, vertical changes bob/squash, airborne lean input biases the angle. Clamped to ±5/±8 u and ±0.25 rad; retry resets it. No physics writes; the head sensor stays on the chassis.

## Suspension struts

Car Feel 3 ([#201](https://github.com/bongohorse/rider/issues/201)): `juice.lua` draws one strut per wheel, a child GO of the chassis. Each strut runs from a visual mount on the chassis centre line, chassis-local (`wheel_x`, 0), to the wheel centre. The wheel-joint anchor (`wheel_x`, −30) is the wheel centre at spring rest, so a strut to it would be invisible. Each render frame the strut is placed at the midpoint, rotated and scaled in y only. Its length is 30 u at spring rest, about 23.5 u at static sag and shorter under compression. The owner calls it from `update` only: it reads GO transforms and never touches bodies, joints or the fixed tick. Created on the first ready frame and kept across Retry. A station switch or proxy unload removes the struts with the world, and a placement failure removes them with the chassis. Edit mode has no vehicle, so no struts. Default on: `?struts=0` or the tuning panel's **Struts** button turns them off, deleting both objects (0 extra sprites). The setting is the module's only state and survives world loads; strut ids live on the vehicle. Budget: +2 sprites/GOs. Compact phones have no tuning panel, so only the URL switch applies there.

## Telemetry, budget and graphs

- `telemetry.lua` is read-only: speed and signed vx, wheel contacts, signed spring travel along chassis Y (positive = compression; no hard stop exists), FPS/frame interval over 0.25 s and `profile.step` (engine ms, not a device benchmark).
- `budget.json` (custom resource): 60 FPS / 16.666667 ms target; physics budget unset until Phase E. Breaches show red. Means can hide stutter.
- `history.lua` keeps at most 301 samples (5 s at 60 Hz) in 50 min/max buckets per signal; retry/preset clears it. Graphs render every bucket spike; FPS colours ≥55 green, 30–54 orange, <30 red.
- Compact HUD (A13b): FPS, speed, contact dots, spring travel and a 200×80 graph; tuning (T) and help (H) start closed; retry reasons are 1.5 s toasts. In narrow portrait tuning hides the graph.
- Debug scenarios (`scenarios.lua`) load only for a configured test; the module is still packaged in release (exclusion is Phase B work).

## Tuning and presets

- `feel.lua` owns all drive/spring/camera values and slider bounds; geometry and bounds are not tuning. Eleven sliders: spring Hz, damping, wheel target, motor cap, air lean, air rate, ground lean, camera response, look-ahead, coast torque, rolling resistance. The owner validates and applies changes live (spring changes wake bodies once).
- During a slider drag gameplay commands are zeroed; held input clears on capture and release.
- `presets/<key>.json` (schema 1) hold complete value sets; `feel.order` (A, B, C, D, E, F) is the registry for the P cycle, chips, `riderHud.presets` and `a9`. A is the measurement baseline, B/C progressively stronger (motor targets above Box2D's ~47 rad/s rotation cap, #114), D ("snappy") and E ("planted") are candidates (#199); F ("D + air damping", #211) is D plus `air_pitch_damping` 8. P cycles; chips select; selection performs a canonical retry. `?preset=d` opens D; an unknown key falls back visibly to A.
- Validation (#199): `feel.validate` checks schema, key = file name, the 22 required `feel.preset_keys` (exactly these, no unknown key), optional keys from `feel.optional` (#211: `air_pitch_damping`, default 0 when absent, so A–E carry none), finite numbers, each value inside its slider range and sign (explicit `feel.ranges` for keys without a slider) and `camera_zoom_min <= camera_zoom`. A refused file falls back to A with a note on the HUD and in the copied report. Applying a preset writes every key, an absent optional key as its default. `tests/bootstrap/test_presets.py` applies the same rules to every file, keeps candidate motor targets under the rotation cap and pins A/B/C byte for byte. Live edits show Modified.

## Audio

`engine.wav` is an original 0.4 s loop (`tools/art/engine_loop.py`, CC0). One voice starts after the first gameplay input, follows average wheel speed (playback 0.65–2.2) and survives retries; M mutes. Never autoplays. Gain/speed are component properties (`go.set`), not `sound.set_speed`.

## Feedback copy

F or Copy feedback formats Markdown with source SHA, preset, station, velocity, contacts, travel, FPS/profile, crash rule, tuning and station results. HTML5 uses `navigator.clipboard.writeText` after the gesture and reports success only when it resolves; otherwise a selectable read-only fallback appears. Native builds show the report. F respects Ctrl/Meta/Alt shortcuts.

## Browser presentation

The page fills the viewport. High DPI ON uses the device pixel ratio, OFF one buffer pixel per CSS pixel (`?dpi=0|1`, reload). Resize, rotation and fullscreen keep the world. Auto Fit keeps world proportions. GUI panel roots own edge anchors and safe-area adjustment; children share their root's FIT space. Slider picking converts `action.screen_x/y` with `gui.screen_to_local`.

## Touch and tilt

- `mobile.js` owns DOM pointer capture, four simultaneous zones, mirrored layouts A/B, the compact touch toolbar and the 30 s capture; `mobile.lua` polls its normalized state into the same commands. Resets, UI capture, blur and rotation clear pointers and keys. Touch unlocks Defold's shared audio context in the trusted gesture. `?touch=1|0` forces the mode.
- `tilt.js` (opt-in, default off) listens after the user enables it, requests permission when needed and maps beta/gamma to a screen-horizontal angle (W3C Z-X-Y). Calibration, full-lean angle, dead zone and inversion map to [-1, 1]; touch/keyboard lean take priority. Retry, UI capture, focus loss and view changes require recalibration. Denial, missing first data (3 s) or invalid samples fall back visibly to touch.
- Browser runners wait for engine acknowledgements before the next press (pitfall 5), including two fresh telemetry updates after fullscreen changes. Phone procedure: [MOBILE_TRIAL.md](../../docs/MOBILE_TRIAL.md).

## Generated art

The game atlas uses Claudio’s `car_body.png`, `car_rider.png`, `car_spring.png` and `car_wheel.png` (upload PRs #246/#247), preserved byte for byte. Atlas aliases keep existing animation IDs. Manual sprite sizes and component offsets fit the art to the existing vehicle; the 48 u wheel sprite is scaled to a 44.15 u tyre diameter (both solid raster diameters within 0.25 u of 44 u), matching its 22 u collision radius. Live drawing order is rider (−0.1), body (0), spring (0.5), wheel (1); replay rider is also behind its body. The rider’s hip pivot/animation and chassis head sensor are unchanged. Springs retain the mount-to-wheel stretch/toggle; their visual top mounts are 8 u above the chassis centre line so the coils remain visible above the foreground tyres.

The earlier generated art is retained as geometry regression fixtures and is no longer packed in the runtime atlas. `tools/art/wheel.py` builds `assets/wheel.svg`, a 3× `wheel.png` (144×144) and `wheel.sprite` (48×48) from the collision radius; Bootstrap CI checks centroid, silhouette diameter against the collision radius and mirror symmetry (`--check` verifies regenerated bytes). Add circular vehicle graphics to `ROUND_ASSETS`. Rider art: `tools/art/rider.py`. `tools/art/strut.py` builds `assets/strut.svg`, a 3× `strut.png` (24×84) and `strut.sprite` (8×28). Its silhouette reaches both ends, so the stretched sprite spans mount to wheel centre (`test_strut_art.py`, `--check`).

## Stations

- `stations/*.json` (schema 1) in world metres: id and name (at most 64 bytes each, as are section ids and names; they travel in every telemetry message), spawn [x, y], `start_speed` (-30..30 m/s), run-in/run-out, safety bounds, right-to-left chain polylines, optional convex CCW hulls (3–8 vertices), `labels` and `sections` (id, spawn, start speed, mode, measurement extents). The whole file validates before terrain creation; unknown ids fall back visibly; bad files fail without replacing the world. Keep segments at least 1 cm.
- `stations.lua` owns selection, data, terrain, markers and unit conversion; the controller starts and retries the vehicle. Start translates all three bodies with the authored speed and matches wheel spin.
- Switching serializes disable/final/unload → `proxy_unloaded` → load → `proxy_loaded` → init → `gym_ready` (15 s failure diagnostic). Preset identity survives; modified sliders reset.
- Selection: keys 1–9, Next station, `?station=<id>&speed=<m/s>&section=<id>`; V / Next section switches sections in the same world; R retries the current section. Test-only stations exist only in the debug runner.
- Release rendering uses ordinary sprites for edges, hulls and ticks (debug lines are absent in release); at most 800 render objects per station; sprite/label pools are 1024.
- Metric observers (`ramp_metrics`, `step_metrics`, `seam_metrics`, `gap_metrics`, `bump_metrics`, `balance_metrics`, `air_metrics`) are read-only and called by the owner. Measurement never stops or resets practice. Results persist across section retries only with matching full tuning and start speed (except `air`, whose jump list restarts with each start or Retry); a live tuning change makes a run non-comparable.

## Ramps

`ramps` (1 m kick ramps) and `ramps-long` (4 m climbs): 5–45° in 5° steps, 60° and a 12 m wall. Up sections start at 8 m/s; `down-<angle>` sections start neutral at 1 m/s. Climb = chassis clears the crest by 0.46 m and stays 0.3 m above the surface; failure after a 1 s stall or 6 s without clearance. Descents report contact share and speed change. Debug `ramp_trial.lua` supplies constant commands from the first step (up: full throttle; down: neutral).

## Steps

`steps` / `steps-down`: 0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5 m, each followed by 12 m flat; independent starts 4 m before the edge at 5 m/s. Results: approach/entry speed, input ranges, peak compression per wheel (window from 0.8 m before the edge), chassis and underside contact (impulse at local y ≤ -13 u). CLIMBED/LANDED needs rear wheel past the edge, chassis above the surface, pitch < 0.8 rad and 0.25 s wheel contact; otherwise STUCK. `section=stop-probe` repeats 0.2 m with disclosed wheel limits [-0.20, +0.18] m; normal sections keep limits disabled.

## Seams

`seams-chain` (key 6) and `seams-hull` (key 7): 20 seams on flat ground, speeds 5/7/9 m/s. Protocol, thresholds and reproduction: [A18 protocol](../../docs/A18_SEAM_PROTOCOL.md).

## Gaps and landings

`gaps` (key 8): 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 m; `gaps-landing` (key 9): 2 m with flat, 15° downslope and 15° upslope landings. Each section starts 2 m before a 15° chain ramp to a 1 m lip over a pit 3 m below ground; default 7 m/s, `&speed=9` for comparison. Protocol (debug `a19`): full throttle, no brake/lean. Takeoff = both wheels past the lip and unloaded; jump distance = first loaded wheel x minus lip x; cleared = both wheels loaded on the landing side with |pitch| < 0.8 rad for 0.25 s; missed = chassis 0.6 m below the deck, 1 s stall or 8 s; a touchdown up to one wheel radius short is `edge_strike`. Repeat tolerances: takeoff 0.02 m/s, distance 0.05 m, compression 5 mm, same status.

## Bumps

- `bumps` (no number key: `?station=bumps` or Next station after `balance`): smooth cosine waves on one chain. Sections `long-<h>` (3 m wavelength, 3 waves) and `short-<h>` (1 m, 6 waves) for h = 0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.3 m (h = trough to crest: y = h/2 (1 − cos 2πx/λ) from ground level), then `crest-dip`: single 0.4 m crests of 8, 6, 4 and 3 m wavelength and dips of the same sizes (curvature rises as the wavelength falls), 6 m flat between them (a dip goes 0.4 m below ground). Each section starts 5 m before its first feature at the default 5 m/s; `&speed=` compares. Labels name height and wavelength.
- Geometry source: `tools/gym/bumps_geometry.cjs` lays out Flat and Bump/wave prefabs as an editor document (`tools/gym/bumps-source.json`); the editor compiler (LE5b sampling, 5 mm / 0.5 m) builds the chain through Import → Export in the debug gym, and the generator adds the station id, kind, sections, labels, bounds and start speed. Regenerate with `node tools/gym/bumps_station.cjs <debug-bundle>`; `a19b` recompiles and fails on a stale file.
- Protocol (debug `a19b`, `bump_metrics.protocol`): constant throttle = start speed / (|motor_speed| × 0.22), the A18 powered control (the wheel target holds the start speed; it saturates at 1 above about 7.04 m/s for preset A), no brake or lean, no tuning change. Each feature has a window from 0.5 m before to 1.5 m after it (chassis centre). Per window: wheel contact loss = completed steps without loaded support (touching or impulsive manifold point, A19 rule) per rear/front wheel, either and both; max compression = positive spring travel from spring rest (static sag about 0.075 m included, reported at window entry), also as a share of 0.38 m, the travel from spring rest until the chassis underside touches flat ground (the joints have no travel stop); chassis pitch min/max; chassis and underbody contact (A17 rule). Crest/dip: apex speed = chassis vx at the apex; lifted = one wheel unsupported for at least 2 consecutive steps in the window; bottomed = underbody contact (impulsive chassis point at chassis-local y ≤ −13 u). A section ends past its last window; 1 s below 0.1 m/s is `STALLED`, 90 s without either is `TIMEOUT` (position recorded), never a reset. A feature never entered has no values; only an independent start replaces an archived result.
- Lift-off speed (`a19b`): `crest-dip` at hold speeds 2.5–7 m/s in 0.5 m/s steps, then three bisection runs per crest between its fastest unlifted and slowest lifted start; reported as the highest apex speed without and the lowest with a lift-off. Repeat tolerances: shares 0.02, compression 5 mm, pitch 0.01 rad, apex speed 0.02 m/s, same lift/bottom status; asserted between two fresh loads of `long-0.3` and of `crest-dip` (both must complete) and for R on `long-0.3`. A stalled section is chaotic, and an in-world reset (V or R) of the 90 m `crest-dip` drifts slightly from a fresh load towards its end, so those differences are recorded, not asserted. CI runs `a19b` in two parts (ladders and repeats; lift-off sweep) and merges them.

## Balance

- `balance` (no number key: `?station=balance` or Next station): seven independent sections, each from rest (`start_speed` 0) on a raised deck 0.8 m above the floor (floor 0.8 m, decks 1.6 m), ending in a target platform with a cliff back to the floor. `beam-2`, `beam-1.5`, `beam-1.2`, `beam-1.05`: a 0.3 m slot, then a beam of that length (both wheel centres, 0.92 m apart, must fit; 1.05 m is at the limit). `up` / `down`: a 0.4 m 35° transition to or from a 2 m platform. `seesaw`: see below. Geometry: `node tools/gym/balance_geometry.cjs` (`--check`; `tests/ci/balance.test.cjs` fails on a stale file).
- Seesaw (first dynamic obstacle): a 4 m × 0.08 m plank (0.3 m tapered ends, 20 kg, friction 0.8, restitution 0, group/mask `gym`), resource `seesaw.go`/`.collisionobject`/`.convexshape`, stated in the station's `seesaw` field and checked by the validator, the CI test and at runtime (`b2d.body.get_mass`). Pivot 0.5 m above the decks on a revolute joint to the terrain body (`collide_connected=true`, so the plank rests on the deck chains; ±25° limit as a safety stop only). At rest its left tip lies on the start deck (14.5°), tipped its right tip on the exit deck. Only kind `balance` may carry a `seesaw`; custom levels cannot, and the editor template copy drops it with the other measurement fields.
- Seesaw ownership (`seesaw.lua`, owner `controller.script`): spawned by factory in `setup`, attached (body borrowed, joint created) with the vehicle, reset with the vehicle on every Retry, section switch and bounds reset in the owner's `fixed_update` window: destroy the joint (old handle must be invalid), deactivate, set body and GO pose (pitfall #1, angle tolerance pitfall #7), zero velocities, reactivate, sleep→wake cycle so the Box2D sleep timer starts at zero (pitfall #9, #217), create a new joint (valid, one joint on the plank, generation + 1). Placement failure deletes the plank object; a world reload leaves cleanup to Defold and `final` drops the references. Section switches keep the terrain body.
- Protocol (debug `a19d`, `balance_metrics.protocol`, declared before evidence runs): completed = both wheel centres over the target with loaded support (A19 rule), |vx| < 0.05 m/s and |pitch| < 0.6 rad held for 1 s; missed = a wheel centre 0.35 m below the target top (fell), |pitch| > 1.6 rad (flipped) or 30 s. Seesaw: rest angle at 0.5 s; tip = plank angle crosses level; settled = |ω| < 0.05 rad/s for 0.25 s after the tip; final angle = the same calm rule after the verdict; `limit_reached` if the joint angle comes within 0.005 rad of the limit; the plank's angle and angular velocity for the first `seesaw_trace_ticks` (60) ticks after each reset are recorded for the retry canonicality check (#217).
- Drivers (`balance_trial.lua`, debug `a19d` only, `window.riderBalanceTrial`): `skill` = closed-loop slow driving with key-like binary throttle/brake (speed reference √(2·0.8·d) capped at 1.0 m/s towards the target centre, ±0.08 m/s band, each key state held ≥ 6 ticks), recorded as a run-length trace; `replay` = that trace open-loop; `full` = throttle held. All start from an in-world R. For an attempt started with `watch_plank: true` the driver also publishes the plank state every fixed tick and latches the first tick after the tip with |ω| > 0.2 (`window.riderPlank`, debug `a19d` only), so the retry probe can catch a seesaw swing shorter than the 0.25 s report interval (#228). The scripted trace is the recorded skill trace of acceptance 1 (Director decision on #57); human driving is Claudio's milestone drive test. Repeat tolerances: stop x 0.05 m, time 0.1 s, tip/settle time 0.05 s, angles 0.01 rad.

## Air control

- `air` (no number key: `?station=air` or Next station): four sections, each a plateau with a 4 m run-in and a 20° kicker (0.5 m rise) to the lip, then a long flight down to the landing area and a 40 m run-out; the next section starts behind a wall. `big` (default): 16 m plateau, 45° downslope from 3 m below the lip with a 15 m radius transition to flat; `mid`: 8 m, 35° downslope from 1.5 m below the lip; `flat`: 8 m onto flat ground; `low`: 3 m onto flat ground. Default 7 m/s; `&speed=` compares. Labels name each section's height and landing angle. Geometry: `node tools/gym/air_station.cjs` writes `stations/air.json` (`--check` compares; `tests/ci/air.test.cjs` and `a19c` fail on a stale file).
- Measurement (`air_metrics`, every jump, also in release): a jump starts on the first step with neither wheel nor the chassis loaded (A19 loaded-support rule), after the first loaded support since the start or Retry (the spawn drop is not a jump), and counts if it lasts at least 0.25 s. A lip takeoff (`from_lip`) has both wheel centres within one wheel radius of the lip or past it at takeoff, or past it later in the same flight (`lip_confirmed_s`; input ranges then start at that step). Airtime = airborne steps until the first loaded wheel or chassis contact. Rotation = summed chassis angle change in flight, positive = backward relative to travel (lean A); flips = whole 360° turns. Landing angle = chassis angle minus the slope of the authored chain segment nearest the first loaded wheel (or the chassis), taken at its closest point (the contact point). Impact = last airborne chassis velocity (vertical and along the ground normal) and peak suspension compression within 0.5 s. LANDED = both wheels loaded with |pitch relative to the ground| < 0.8 rad for 0.25 s within 2 s; CRASHED = the chassis touches first or no LANDED within 2 s; `chassis_contact` reports chassis contact within the 0.5 s impact window (bottoming); `complete` = status final and that window closed. The HUD shows the current flight, then the run's lip jump (later landing bounces only add a count); the report keeps the last 12 jumps since the start or Retry; under A20 slow motion each jump is also marked not comparable.
- Protocol (debug `a19c`, `air_trial.lua`): full throttle, no lean until the lip takeoff (both wheel centres past the lip); then neutral drive with lean −1, 0 or +1 (`&lean=`) held for the whole flight, then neutral. Every section with all three leans at 7 m/s, `?station=air` alone, `&speed=9`, 6.3 and 5.6 with lean +1 on `big` (takeoff tick phase). At 7 m/s on `big`, full back lean must reach at least +360° and full front lean −360° (acceptance). Repeat tolerances: takeoff speed 0.02 m/s, airtime 0.034 s (two steps), rotation 5°, landing angle 5°, compression 5 mm, same status; on fresh loads (`big` +1 and −1, `low` 0, `?station=air` against `big` 0) and through in-world R.

## Custom levels

- `?station=custom` loads the last valid imported level, or shows the Import panel. **I** or **Import level** (page button; in touch mode the toolbar button) imports a station JSON file or pasted JSON (schema 1, no `kind`). Rules, save files and limits: [level-editor contract](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#custom-level-runtime-and-save-le1a).
- The loader (`boot.script` + `custom.lua`) validates, freezes a snapshot and reloads the world; the world reads the snapshot by id. Retry, bounds reset, keys 1–9 and presets behave as for built-in stations. Custom levels never get the Straight checker background.
- Samples for drive tests in `samples/`: `rolling-hills` (chain-only, seven 1.2 m hills), `kicker-platform` (15° kicker, 2 m pit gap, landing on a hull platform, start 7 m/s) and `invalid-bounds`, which must fail with `Import failed: Invalid safety bounds. The current level and its save are unchanged.` `seg-800` and `len-99km` are the largest LE1b levels (generated by `tools/gym/levels.cjs`, checked by `tests/ci/levels.test.cjs`).
- The feedback report (F / **Copy report**) has a `Level load` line: trigger → drive-ready switch, parse, terrain and decoration times in whole milliseconds (`<1 ms` below the HTML5 clock step, `n/a` without a trigger such as a page load) plus terrain shapes and decoration sprites/labels. Measurements: [LE1b report](../../docs/LE1B_REPORT.md).

## Level editor

- **E** on any station opens the editor: a built-in station as a template copy (`my-<id>`), a custom level as itself. **E**, **Enter** or **Play** compiles and drives from START; **E** while driving returns to the editor view; **R** only works while driving. Rules and controls: [level-editor contract](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#editor-shell-le2).
- Drive test: open `ramps`, press **E**, drag START, choose **FINISH** and click on the ground, press **E** and drive through the flag (`Finish reached` once per attempt). Back in the editor, point at the terrain and press **F** for a temporary test start (Shift+F drops at the cursor).
- **Draw [D]** places snapped points (0.5 m grid, Alt 0.1 m, Shift 15°, Ctrl off, G grid on/off); click the last point or press Enter to finish. Starting or ending on a free line end (green ring) connects the pieces; a connected run becomes one chain. Drag a line to move its run, drag a point to reshape; a moved exit point carries the downstream run along. Double-click inserts a point, K splits, J connects/disconnects (Shift+click a second free end to bridge a gap with a new piece), Tab cycles what is under the cursor. Overhangs and curls are allowed: a run that turns back in x collides on both sides. Rules: [draw tool and connections](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#draw-tool-and-connections-le3).
- LE3 drive test: draw a run-up, a ramp starting at its end, a gap and a landing; place FINISH; drive it. Then drag the joint between run-up and ramp (the ramp follows), Undo/Redo, and drive again.
- **My levels (LE4):** **New** starts an empty level, **Open** lists My levels (newest first; Delete asks once more) and the built-in templates, **Rename level** (right panel, nothing selected) edits the name. Every edited level is saved in the browser; a template or file copy joins My levels at its first edit. **Export** downloads the playable level as JSON (Play-blocking errors block it too); **Import** opens a file as a copy. **Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V** copy a selection and paste it at the cursor (connections inside the copy are kept). Rules: [document management](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#document-management-le4).
- **Prefabs (LE5a, LE5b):** **Prefab [P]** places Flat, Ramp, Step (riser and tread), Gap with pit, Kicker + landing, Platform and Bump/wave (keys 1–7 or the list); placing one on a free end (green ring) connects it. Select a prefab to change its values in the right panel (click a field, type, Enter) or with its pink handles; the entry stays put and everything downstream moves along. **Mirror [<] [>]** places a mirrored copy of the selection to the left or right. Rules: [prefabs](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#prefabs-le5a).
- LE5a drive test: build a ten-object prefab course, change a connected ramp, Undo/Redo, save/reopen and drive.
- **Curves (LE5b):** **Curve [C]** places anchors like Draw (tangents follow automatically); select the curve to drag its anchors and tangents (pink), switch an anchor's tangents between free, smooth and symmetric in the right panel, double-click to add an anchor, Delete to remove it, K to split. Curves and Bump/wave are sampled to 5 mm / 0.5 m, independent of the zoom. Rules: [curves and sampling](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#curves-bumpwave-and-sampling-le5b).
- LE5b drive test: build and drive a smooth hill (Bump/wave) and a valley (curve), change their values, Undo/Redo and reopen the saved course.
- **Corner / Smooth (LE5c):** select a line, click one of its points (or a joint) and press **Make smooth**: the corner is rounded through the same point and gets pink tangent handles; **Make corner** makes it sharp again. Prefabs first need **Convert to editable path** (asks once more; the shape and connections stay, the parameter fields go). Rules: [editable paths](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#editable-paths-corner-and-smooth-points-le5c).
- **Several points and whole objects (LE5d):** select a line or curve, then drag a box on empty space to select its points, or Shift+click points to add or remove them (Esc first to box-select objects instead). **Make smooth** / **Make corner** apply to every selected point at once, or to all points of the selected objects when no point is picked (prefabs are converted first after **Sure? Make smooth**). Dragging one selected point moves all of them. A converted Bump/wave is a curve through its crests and troughs. Rules: [several points](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#several-points-and-whole-objects-le5d).
- **Warnings and navigation (LE6a):** the warning button lists every error and warning; click a row with `-> show` to select its source and centre it (rows without a place only show their text). The View row at the bottom: **Fit [Home]** shows the whole level, **Centre [.]** the selection, **START** and **Last test** (the last test start that drove in this level) jump there, **Bounds** shows the safety bounds Play and Export use. None of them changes the level or adds an Undo step. The Open dialog pages the templates (`More >`, or the wheel). Rules: [diagnostics and navigation](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#diagnostics-and-navigation-le6a).
- **Labels, help and preview (LE6b):** **Label [L]** places a label and lets you type its text at once (Enter applies, 64 bytes); double-click a label or use **Text** on the right to change it later; drag, copy/paste, Mirror and Delete work as for other objects. **H** (or **Help [H]**) shows every control; nothing behind it reacts until it is closed (H, Esc, Close or a click outside). Over empty ground the faint vehicle (**Ghost**) shows where **F** would start (red: refused, with the reason); switch it off with **Ghost**. Rules: [labels, help and readability](../../docs/contracts/level-editor.md#labels-help-and-readability-le6b).
