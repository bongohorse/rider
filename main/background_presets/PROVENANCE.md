# Original environment presets

Every pixel is generated by `tools/art/background_presets.py` using Pillow from the pinned development requirements. No archive, external image, reference still or third-party asset is an input. The original terrain material families supply names only.

Run `python tools/art/background_presets.py --write` to reproduce all outputs; `--check` verifies the committed files without a source archive. `manifest.json` records generator/file/raw RGBA hashes, dimensions, stable IDs and fixed seeds. The generator contains all palettes and geometry inputs. The licence is in `License.txt`.

Each of Meadow, Quarry, City and Volcano has five 1024×512 RGBA background layers, one 1024×512 RGBA foreground and a 256×128 composited thumbnail. Layer 1 is opaque sky; layers 2–5 and foreground have transparent upper regions. Horizontal endpoints match after antialiasing. Vertical edge pixels, including alpha, extend under the runtime's clamp rule. Meadow's foreground is authored for #603's full-height, bottom-anchored display.

Meadow (#606) uses a separate motif per depth: cumulus sky, snow-capped far peaks, farmland with hedges and barns, mixed forest, a near meadow with bushes, fence and flowers, and a low grass and fern foreground. The other presets stay unchanged until Claudio accepts this direction.

The other sets use muted distant shapes and stronger near silhouettes: cut stone, an original city skyline, and volcanic rock with small amber fissures. All presets are static environment art, with no weather or animation. Claudio judges art direction in #549 and, for Meadow, #606; pixel checks establish technical integrity only. Kenney test fixtures are separate and remain unchanged.
